## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-07

### Added

- Initial public release of the GaussWeave reproducibility toolkit.
- Typed configuration, scene, Gaussian, rendering, accounting, and result modules.
- Deterministic Blender helpers and opt-in CUDA/gsplat workflows.
- Unit, integration, GPU, packaging, and repository-quality checks.
